Privacy Policy: Papers for Peace of Mind Publisher

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Papers for Peace of Mind Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Papers for Peace of Mind Etsy shop (https://www.etsy.com/shop/PapersForPeaceOfMind) to create and schedule Pins of her own products on her own Pinterest boards.

What the app accesses

  • The app connects only to the shop owner's own Pinterest account, with her permission.
  • It reads her boards and creates or schedules Pins on them.
  • It does not access, collect or store information about any other Pinterest user.

What is stored

  • A Pinterest access token for the owner's account, stored securely on a private server and used only to create Pins.
  • No personal data about other people is collected, sold or shared.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings, which stops the app working immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the shop's Etsy messages at https://www.etsy.com/shop/PapersForPeaceOfMind.
