A just-in-case plan for your pets and horses (UK)

By Hope, who works in probate in the UK · Updated 11 November 2026

Our animals depend on us completely. If you were taken into hospital tonight, would someone know who feeds them, what they're allergic to and which vet to call?

For an emergency

  • Name one or two emergency carers who've agreed to step in, and give them your plan.
  • Write down where your animals are, how to get in (keys, gate and yard codes) and who runs the yard.
  • List your vet, the out-of-hours number and your insurer.
  • Tell your vet who your emergency carer is, and whether they can authorise treatment if you can't be reached.
  • Keep a card in your purse or wallet saying your animals need care, with a contact number.

Each animal's routine

  • Food, amounts and times, exercise or turnout, and rugging for horses.
  • Health conditions, allergies, medication and anything they're afraid of.
  • For horses: the passport number or UELN and microchip number, and where the passport is kept. All horses, ponies and donkeys in England must be microchipped.

The longer term

  • Who you'd like to care for each animal if you couldn't, and whether you've asked them. A horse can be a 25-year commitment.
  • How their costs would be met. A gift of an animal in a Will is usually made to a person, ideally with money for their care.
  • Your wishes for a horse's future: keep, loan, retire or rehome.

Questions

Can I leave my horse to someone in my Will?

Yes. It's usually done as a gift to a person, ideally with money for the horse's care. Take professional advice when making your Will.

What should an emergency card say?

That you have animals that need care, where they are, and who to call.

Does a care plan need to be legal?

No. It's information and wishes. Anything you want to be binding belongs in your Will.

This guide is general information for England and Wales. It is not legal, financial or veterinary advice. Rules can change, so check GOV.UK or speak to a qualified professional about your situation.
