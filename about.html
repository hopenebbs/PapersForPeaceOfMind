About

Hello, I'm Hope

I work in probate in the UK. Every week I sit with families who are trying to piece together a life after someone has died. Which bank? Which pension? Where is the Will? Was there a password to the phone? Grief is hard enough without a treasure hunt.

I've also spent my life around horses and yards, where there's a whole other list of things only the owner knows: who feeds what, which vet, where the passport is.

Papers for Peace of Mind is the set of planners I wish every family had. They're written for UK life, designed to be calm and beautiful to use, and every page includes practical insight from the probate desk or the yard.

Our planners organise information. They aren't a Will or legal advice, and for anything complex we always suggest speaking to a qualified professional.
